What we believe

Financial freedom is just the starting point.

It isn't about adding one more zero to your bank account. Once money stops being the problem, you get your time back, and you get to choose what to do with it.

01

Build your second engine.

Most people optimise their time, not their money. Your work is one engine. Investing that compounds is the second. Like a plane, take-off burns the most fuel. At altitude, compounding does the heavy lifting, and unlike savings, it won't run dry if you live past 90.

02

Freedom, then what?

First, freedom from the pressure of work and from anyone else controlling your time. Then, freedom to give your time, expertise, wisdom and money to the people and causes you care about. That's where purpose lives.

03

Be the change in your family tree.

Our parents never taught us about money. They did what they could. We can be the change for the generations that follow, and so can you.

You don't need to change a million lives in your lifetime.

Teach your children, and they'll teach theirs. Over a few generations, that's how a million lives change, long after we're gone.

If you won't do it for yourself, do it for your kids. No school will teach them this. It's on us.

Questions

Good things to ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Starter Community is free, and so are the checklist and the Financial Freedom Fundamentals series inside it. When you're ready to go further, Mastering Money is a monthly membership, and our investing and options courses are paid. You can decide on those later, or never.

Do I need to know anything about investing?

No. We start with personal finance, before any investing. Begin where you are.

Will you tell me what to buy?

No. We don't give stock tips, price targets or trade signals. We teach you how to think it through so you can decide for yourself. Everything here is education, not individual investment advice.

Am I too old, or too young, to start?

No. It's never too early or too late. The fundamentals are the same at 15 or 65, and the best time to start is now. We both started from nothing too.

What happens after I join?

You'll start in the Starter Community with the free checklist and the Financial Freedom Fundamentals series. Introduce yourself, ask questions and go at your own pace. When you're ready, move up to Mastering Money and join our live Q&A each month.
